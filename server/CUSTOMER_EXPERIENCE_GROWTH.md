# Customer 360, feedback and growth

This phase extends existing Core bookings, vendor commitments, customer consent, VendorReview and notification delivery. The customer view is at `/customer/customer-360`; vendor views are `/vendor/customers`, `/vendor/experience-qr`, and `/vendor/growth`. Admin controls are under `/admin/experience` and enforce separate API permissions.

Customer records are grouped by platform account only for owned Core bookings or explicitly claimed feedback with verified mobile and active ecosystem permission. External private contacts do not automatically merge, export, or become campaign recipients. Names remain self-reported. Customer-specific vendor marketing permission, account-level channel preference and active account state are checked before in-app campaign delivery; campaigns are frequency-limited to one per customer per seven days across vendors.

Feedback QR tokens contain random secrets rather than customer details, are hashed in storage, expire after 30 days and can be rotated or revoked. Recoverable vendor copies are encrypted using the configured external JWT secret; rotating that secret makes old copies unrecoverable, so issue fresh QR codes. Booking ownership, completion and recipient phone are checked before issuance; the recipient verifies the same mobile using the existing OTP provider. External completion remains vendor-reported and the review establishes recipient confirmation, not Core payment or booking authority. Customers can review without registration. Optional permissions do not enable campaigns on their own; account linking additionally requires verified account identity and ecosystem permission. One review per booking, revision checks, a seven-day correction window, reports, reasoned moderation and histories apply. Private photos are authenticated Cloudinary assets and require separate admin media access.

Optional physical-card requests record a delivery-only address consent; fulfillment and tracking are manual admin processes. A pending request can be cancelled and its address removed. Support and guest deletion requests are recorded for review. Guest privacy processing removes stored name, written feedback and address and revokes the link, but provider media deletion remains a separately verified operation.

Referral attribution requires two independently verified consenting customer accounts, rejects self-referrals and cannot automatically award rewards. Versioned loyalty rules are admin-controlled and award non-cash points only for verified completed and paid Core bookings; reconciliation is idempotent and reverses awards when eligibility is lost. Optional interest suggestions use the customer's own saved records and personalization permission. No AI provider or campaign is invoked automatically.

Rollout requires configured MSG91 and authenticated Cloudinary delivery, production indexes for the new collections and the partial unique review index, deployment, operational review of retention and permission assignments, and live end-to-end verification. The customer directory currently returns at most the latest 500 bookings per source. Loyalty reconciliation runs when the customer requests refresh, rather than from a background event worker. Live OTP, Cloudinary, physical fulfillment and production migration have not been exercised by local tests; local provider integration tests use fixtures or mocks.
