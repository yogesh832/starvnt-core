# STARVNT legal and privacy release review

Review opened 7 November 2026. Scope follows the two release phases supplied by the product owner. This is an engineering review and a decision brief for legal, privacy, product and finance reviewers. Approval evidence is pending.

## Phase 1 before signup release

| Item | Current evidence | Release decision and evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customer and vendor signup documents | The web [legal page](../web/src/pages/PublicPolicyPage.jsx) and native [legal screen](../mobile/src/screens/LegalNotice.tsx) show publication placeholders. The native [auth layout](../mobile/src/AuthLayout.tsx) hides legal links on registration and verification screens. | Approve the customer platform terms, the applicable vendor signup terms, and the privacy notice. Confirm the contracting entity, business model, support and grievance contacts, and effective date. Publish approved versions and make the applicable documents readable before account creation. |
| Signup acceptance | The reviewed [external auth routes](../server/src/external/routes/auth.routes.js) create accounts through registration and first-time identity flows. The reviewed routes do not record an approved signup document version and affirmative acceptance. | Confirm the required acknowledgement for each role. Enforce it on the server across email, Google and mobile OTP account creation where enabled. Record the account, role, document version and hash, and server timestamp. Decide how existing accounts are handled when approved terms take effect. |
| Optional permissions and privacy rights | [Consent service](../server/src/foundation/consent.service.js) records versioned decisions separately for ecosystem linking, personalization and marketing. [Privacy settings](../web/src/pages/customer/PrivacySettings.jsx) support withdrawal and deletion requests. | Approve the data and purpose inventory, recipients and processors, retention periods, rights and grievance handling. Verify signup works without optional permissions and withdrawal applies on all enabled channels. Review account and vendor data access alongside profile verification. |
| Profile photos and processor retention | [Photo upload](../server/src/customer/routes/profilePicture.routes.js) stores a Cloudinary URL. Removing a photo or [anonymizing an account](../server/src/foundation/privacy.service.js) clears the account URL. Neither path deletes the corresponding Cloudinary asset. | Confirm the disclosure, access model, and retention or erasure policy for Cloudinary media. Verify deletion or documented retention for uploaded photos, including replacements. Confirm the notice also covers enabled OTP, identity, analytics and AI processors as applicable. |

**Signup approval record** — approved document links, roles covered, version and hash, effective date, legal and privacy approvers, publication evidence, and web/native signup acceptance results. Signoff should cover the actual release configuration.

## Phase 2 before paid booking release

| Item | Current evidence | Release decision and evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Advance and cancellation/refunds | [Policy resolver](../server/src/common/policyResolver.js) resolves effective approved versions. [Operations](../server/src/admin/routes/operations.routes.js) uses the booking refund snapshot for applicable refunds. | Legal and Finance approve the advance base, calculation and rounding, refund tiers and cutoffs, cancellation by each party, exceptions, dispute process and refund timelines. The PDF's 20% advance and refund tiers remain drafts. |
| Payout review and settlement | [Completion validation](../server/src/admin/services/executionSettlement.service.js) computes payout review eligibility from verified completion and the saved delay. A missing delay currently falls back to zero in that calculation. Release and eligibility are separate steps. | Approve the trigger, calendar or business days, holds, disputes, fees/tax treatment and provider reconciliation. Test records with missing or legacy snapshots before allowing settlement. The PDF's three-day eligibility review remains a draft and describes a review point, not a transfer commitment. |
| Two-person approval and policy history | [Policy approval](../server/src/admin/routes/policy.routes.js) rejects self-approval and checks the submitted policy hash. [Quote/payment routes](../server/src/external/routes/transaction.routes.js) carry the financial policy snapshot into linked records and check for an approved snapshot on the advance route. | Verify independent approvers, effective date boundaries and preservation of the accepted quote's policy after policy changes. Retain approval and audit evidence for the activated version. |
| Customer agreement and payment authorization | The reviewed [quote advance endpoint](../server/src/external/routes/transaction.routes.js#L910) checks financial policy status before provider order creation. It does not check a separate booking-specific agreement acceptance or authorization for the exact amount in that handler. | Approve the applicable booking and vendor agreements and the authorization wording. Require server validation across paid entry points before creating an order. Preserve the accepted documents, quote/booking reference, exact amount and currency, and acceptance evidence. Material changes require renewed acceptance where applicable. |

**Paid booking approval record** — approved payment, cancellation, refund and settlement terms, activated policy version and effective date, separate author and approver, applicable signed-off agreements, and staging evidence for acceptance, policy snapshots, refunds and settlement.

## Sources and boundaries

- The available product reference, `C:/Users/Hp/Downloads/main (16).pdf`, pp. 241–246, describes separate account terms, booking agreements, optional permissions and exact payment authorization. Its engineering phase labels differ from the two release phases in this review. The draft financial values are recorded from the product owner's instructions. The older handbook PDF named in the previous repository audit was unavailable at its recorded path.
- India's DPDP Act and Rules have staggered commencement. The November 2025 Gazette schedules the core notice and consent provisions reviewed here for the 18-month commencement tranche. The signup release gate in this brief follows the product owner's instruction to review earlier. [Act commencement notification](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/c56ceae6c383460ca69577428d36828b.pdf) · [DPDP Rules notification](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf)
- Counsel should confirm how the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules apply to STARVNT's business model. Rule 4 addresses affirmative purchase consent, cancellation-charge conditions and accepted refunds. [Official E-Commerce Rules, 2020](https://consumeraffairs.nic.in/sites/default/files/E%20commerce%20rules_0.pdf)
- Findings describe the local repository. Live legal pages, release settings, approved contracts and production payment configuration were not verified.
