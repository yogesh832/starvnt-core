# Meeting summary evidence and retry recovery — 17 November 2026

Partial V24.02 and V64.02 hardening in the existing meeting workflow. Summary edits atomically append the previous known summary/resources and newly saved text, actor, reason, timestamp, revision and content hash while updating the current Vendor-facing content. The first observed legacy summary is captured on edit; missing older text is not invented. Vendor reads continue to expose only current authorized meeting content and their own participation; staff history remains behind the existing meeting permissions.

The schedule/cancellation/summary commands now hash their normalized operation, actor, prior revision and reason. Repeating the same command at the immediately following revision recovers its result without another history event. Different content/reason/actor at that prior revision conflicts. Concurrent identical summary saves recover the same committed revision. This is immediate revision-bound recovery, not a permanent outbox: later commands can advance the revision, and schedule validation can reject a retry after the original start time passes. Those broader recovery cases remain open.
